Tractor Hay Haul – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Tractor Hay Haul (the "App"), published by the developer of Tractor Hay Haul ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Android app (package com.lawevanp.tractorhayhaul) and to the browser version of the game.

Summary

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect or transmit any personal or device information to us or to anyone else. The Android app requests no device permissions, including no internet permission.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small amount of game data locally on your device:

  • your coin balance and which tractor colors you have unlocked,
  • your selected tractor color, and
  • your music and sound-effect volume settings.

This data never leaves your device through the App and is never sent to us. On Android it may be included in your device's own Google backup if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. In the browser version it is kept in your browser's local storage. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the App.

Google Play and other stores

When you download the App from Google Play, Google may collect information (for example, to process the download, provide crash reports or measure installs) under its own terms. We do not receive personal information from Google about you. See the Google Privacy Policy. If you play the browser version on itch.io, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to that website.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it does not collect any personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
